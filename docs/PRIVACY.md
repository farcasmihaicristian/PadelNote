# PadelNote Watch Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 4 October 2026

PadelNote Watch (“the app”) is a padel scoring and journal app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is used.

## Data the app uses

### Match and journal data
Match scores, player names you enter, reflection answers, and related stats are stored **on your devices** (and may sync between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch via WatchConnectivity). We do not operate a PadelNote cloud account that receives this data.

### Sign in with Apple
If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple provides an identifier (and optionally a name/email you choose to share) so the app can associate your local profile. That sign-in is handled by Apple; we do not sell this information.

### Health and fitness (Apple Watch)
During a match on Apple Watch, the app may record a workout and read related metrics such as heart rate and active energy through **HealthKit**, with your permission. Workout data is written to Apple Health on your devices. The iPhone app may check Health authorization status related to those Watch workouts; it does not independently record padel workouts.

### Purchases (PadelNote Watch Pro)
Optional subscriptions are processed by **Apple** through the App Store / StoreKit. Apple handles payment details. The app only receives entitlement status (whether Pro is active) to unlock features such as themes, serve-indicator style, and full match history.

## What we do not do
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not use your HealthKit data for advertising or sell it to advertising platforms.
- We do not require an account to score matches.

## Sharing
Data stays on your devices and with Apple services you already use (Health, App Store, Sign in with Apple), except where you choose to share a match or screenshot yourself.

## Your choices
- You can revoke Health access in the Health app.
- You can stop using Sign in with Apple or delete the app to remove local data from that device.
- You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information about children.

## Contact
For privacy questions about PadelNote Watch, contact the developer via the App Store product page support / contact information for this app.

## Changes
We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest revision.
